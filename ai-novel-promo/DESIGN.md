# 墨境宣传片视觉规范

## Style Prompt

墨境的宣传片采用“暗色编辑室 + 纸张工艺 + 精密数据线”的视觉方向：像一间深夜写作工作室，也像一套可靠的长篇创作控制台。画面应有纸页、黄铜、墨黑、淡玉色状态点和克制的界面线条，强调“灵感被整理成可持续推进的长篇作品”。

## Colors

- Background: `#080706` deep ink black
- Foreground: `#f3ead9` warm manuscript ivory
- Muted text: `#b8aa92` aged paper gray
- Accent: `#c99547` brass amber
- Secondary accent: `#83a78c` muted jade
- Dark panel: `#15120e` smoked walnut

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`, fallback `Songti SC`, `serif`
- Data and labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, fallback `PingFang SC`, `monospace`
- Body: `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `sans-serif`

Display type is large, calm, and editorial. Mono labels carry product facts and counters. Chinese text should feel precise, not playful.

## Motion Rules

- Scene entrances are staggered and directional, with a slow camera push on image layers.
- Primary transition: warm blur crossfade, because the story continues between scenes.
- Topic change transition: brass vertical wipe blocks for feature sections.
- No element exits before a transition, except the final closing fade.
- Ambient motion should be subtle: drifting paper grain, fine lines, tiny jade status points.

## What NOT To Do

- No purple-blue neon AI dashboard.
- No generic centered card-grid SaaS layout.
- No gradient text.
- No readable text inside generated image assets.
- No frantic kinetic typography; this is premium, reliable, author-focused.
